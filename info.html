Hello all, I am writing this blog about: “The Sunrise On The Veld,” a short story about a young boy coming to terms with his mortality, written by Doris Lessing. When I started reading this story, I was unsure what to expect since I had never read something like this before, especially not in a language that was not my own. What I found particularly difficult was understanding the symbolism behind the whole story, and I am not ashamed to admit that I have done quite a bit of googling to get a better grip on what it all meant. But before getting into the deeper meaning, I want to focus on the reading experience itself. The writing style pleasantly surprised me. Even though the language was sometimes difficult for me to understand, it described the landscape and scenery very well. Making it a pleasant reading experience.
