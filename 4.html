After reading the entire story and reflecting on it for a while, I can say that The Sunrise on the Veld surprised me much more than I expected. When I first started, I thought it would just be a simple story about a boy and his morning adventure. Instead, it turned into a very deep exploration of what it means to grow up and face reality. I really appreciate how Doris Lessing manages to mix the beauty of nature with the harshness of life in such a short number of pages. It makes the reader slow down and think about things we usually avoid, like suffering and our own limitations. Overall this was quite a good read, and it has definitely redefined the definition of a short story to me.
